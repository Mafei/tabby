Tabby portable Linux terminal font bundle
========================================

Tabby redistributes these font files without changing their bytes, outlines,
internal names, Unicode mappings or hinting. Local CSS family aliases are
application settings only. Font copyrights and licenses remain with their
respective owners. This bundle does not grant trademark or endorsement rights.
The manifest identifies the exact source revision and SHA-256 of every file.

Legacy SourceCodePro.ttf
-----------------------
The repository's pre-existing SourceCodePro.ttf is not one of the pinned files
in this manifest. It has embedded SauceCodePro NF metadata and an OFL license.
Its historical Nerd Fonts patch revision has not been established by this
work; it must not be represented as the new pinned JetBrains font bundle.
